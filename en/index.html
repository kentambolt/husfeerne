Frequently asked questions

Questions about cleaning in Aalborg?

What does cleaning cost in Aalborg?

At Husfeerne regular cleaning always costs DKK 325/hour incl. VAT (260 ex VAT) – for home cleaning, deep cleaning or office cleaning alike. Special jobs such as stairwell cleaning, window cleaning, sofa cleaning and car interior cleaning are quoted at a fixed price up front. No fees, no surprises.

Who actually comes to clean?

Husfeerne is a small all-female team, and it is the same two people who come to your home every time. They are trained in techniques, products and protective equipment, they are insured, and you have direct contact with them if anything needs changing. If someone else has to come on a rare occasion, we tell you well in advance.

Is Husfeerne a cleaning company or a platform?

Husfeerne is a small cleaning company in Aalborg with our own trained employees – not a platform that matches you with private helpers. Our staff are covered by our business and liability insurance, and it is the same two people who come every time.

Do you speak languages other than Danish?

Yes. Husfeerne serves you in Danish, English, German and Ukrainian – on the phone, by email and on this website, where you can switch language in the menu at the top. Feel free to write to us in whichever language you are most comfortable with.

Can I get the service deduction (servicefradrag)?

Yes. Cleaning in your own home qualifies for the Danish service deduction – in 2026 up to DKK 18,300 per adult in the household, with a tax value of roughly 26%. That brings the effective price down to around DKK 240/hour. You simply pay electronically and report the amount to Skat; we send you an invoice with everything you need.

Do you take on tasks that aren't on your list?

Almost always. We believe we can help with nearly any cleaning task. If we lack the equipment, we buy it; if the job requires a course or certification, we take it. Describe your task and we'll give you an honest answer and a fixed price.

How does sofa and textile cleaning work?

We use a professional extraction machine: it sprays clean water and a gentle, eco-friendly detergent deep into the fabric and immediately vacuums it back out together with dirt, stains, odours and allergens. Most sofas are dry again within 4–8 hours. We clean in your home – no need to move anything.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Aalborg, and travel is free throughout Aalborg Municipality – e.g. Hasseris, Vejgaard, Gug, Skalborg, Aalborg Øst, Nørresundby and Svenstrup. We also cover all of North Jutland, including Hjørring, Frederikshavn, Thisted, Brønderslev and Hobro; outside the municipality we agree a fee up front.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No – we bring everything, including eco-friendly, Nordic Swan-labelled products and professional equipment. It's all included in the price.

Is there a binding period?

No. You can cancel or pause your cleaning at any time – no strings attached.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within 2–5 business days of your call. Move-out cleaning can often be arranged faster – just ask.

What is included in a regular cleaning?

Kitchen, bathroom, vacuuming and floor washing, dusting of furniture, skirting boards and trims, mirrors and glass surfaces, and emptying bins. Bed linen and towels are changed by arrangement. All products and equipment are included in the price.

See the full checklist for home cleaning
Can I move or cancel a cleaning?

Yes. You can move or cancel a booked cleaning free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed time. If you cancel later, we may invoice up to 50% of the booked time. Regular agreements can always be cancelled without notice.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry full business and liability insurance, and every employee is trained in techniques, products and protective equipment.
