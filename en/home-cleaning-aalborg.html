Price

What does cleaning help cost in Aalborg?

You always get a fixed price before we start – and it never changes afterwards.

Home cleaning costs DKK 325/hour incl. VAT (DKK 260 ex VAT). All products and equipment are included, and there is a minimum of 3 hours per visit. Travel is free within Aalborg Municipality; outside the municipality we agree a travel fee up front.

Cleaning in your own home qualifies for the Danish service deduction (servicefradrag) – in 2026 up to DKK 18,300 per adult in the household, with a tax value of roughly 26%. That brings your effective price down to around DKK 240/hour. You pay electronically and report the amount to Skat, and we send an invoice with everything you need.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about home cleaning

What does home cleaning cost in Aalborg?

DKK 325/hour incl. VAT – approx. DKK 240/hour after the Danish service deduction (servicefradrag). A 3-hour visit, which is the minimum, thus costs DKK 975 incl. VAT and approx. DKK 720 after the deduction. Products and equipment are included in the price, and travel within Aalborg Municipality is free.

How many hours do I need?

An ordinary 70–90 m² flat in, say, Vestbyen or Vejgaard is typically done in 3 hours, a 130–160 m² house in Hasseris or Gug in 4–5 hours. We recommend a number of hours based on your home – and we say so if we think you can manage with less.

How often should you come?

That's up to you: weekly, bi-weekly or as needed. You get a fixed day and time with the same two cleaners, and you can always change the rhythm or pause it.

Can I get the service deduction (servicefradrag)?

Yes. Cleaning in your own home is covered by the Danish service deduction (servicefradrag). You need to do three things: pay the invoice electronically (not in cash), keep it, and report the labour cost on skat.dk under Servicefradrag. We itemise the labour cost on the invoice, so the amount is ready to enter. The limit and tax value are in the price box above.

Do I need to be home when you come?

That's up to you. Many people are at work while we clean. If we need a key or a code, we always put a written agreement in place first.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We bring everything – Nordic Swan Ecolabel-certified products and professional equipment – so you don't need to keep anything at home, and it is included in the hourly rate. If you have wishes for specific products, e.g. for a special floor, just tell us when we arrange the cleaning.

Is there a binding period – and what if I need to cancel once?

No, there is no binding period and no subscription. If you want to pause over the holidays or stop altogether, just let us know. A single cleaning can be moved or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed time; if you cancel later, we may invoice up to 50% of the booked time (see the terms & conditions).

How quickly can you start?

Usually within 2–5 business days. We call you within 24 hours on weekdays, agree the hours and a fixed day – and the first cleaning is in the calendar.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Then you call us, and we come back for free and put it right. That is our satisfaction guarantee.
