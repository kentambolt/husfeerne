Frequently asked questions

Questions about commercial and office cleaning in Aalborg

What does commercial cleaning cost in Aalborg?

DKK 260/hour ex VAT (DKK 325 incl. VAT). Three hours of cleaning once a week therefore costs DKK 780 ex VAT per visit, and products and equipment are included in the price. If you would rather pay the same amount every month, we set a fixed monthly price based on the agreed scope.

What is included in office cleaning?

Desks and touch points, meeting rooms and corridors, kitchen and coffee station, toilets, glass doors and mirrors, plus waste handling. Floors are vacuumed and washed. All products and equipment are included in the price. Window cleaning and carpet cleaning can be added as regular or one-off jobs.

Can you come outside our opening hours?

Yes. We are happy to come before opening or after closing, so the cleaning does not disturb your work. We can hold a key, code or alarm details under a written agreement.

Is our office too small for regular office cleaning?

No. We are happy to take small business customers – office cleaning of two offices, a shared kitchen and a toilet is a perfectly good agreement, and that is often how a partnership starts.

Who comes to us?

The same two trained and insured cleaners every time. They get to know your premises and your procedures, and you have direct contact with them.

Is there a binding period on the agreement?

No. The scope can be scaled up or down, and the agreement can be cancelled whenever it suits your business. A single visit can be moved or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed time.

How quickly can you start?

We come by and look at the premises, and you get a written quote. After that we can usually start within 2–5 business days.

Do you also clean after tradespeople?

Yes. Construction cleaning and property services are part of what we do – both the rough clean-up along the way and the final cleaning before the premises are put into use.
