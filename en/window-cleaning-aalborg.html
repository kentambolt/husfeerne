The method

Why a pure-water system

It is not just a bucket and a cloth. The method is the reason the result lasts.

No soap residue

The water is deionised, so all minerals and salts are filtered out. When it dries, it leaves nothing behind – neither limescale spots nor soap streaks.

No ladders against the facade

The telescopic pole reaches windows several storeys up from the ground. It is faster, it is safer for us, and it leaves no marks on your facade or your plants.

Sills and frames included

The water runs down onto the sill and takes the dirt with it. We wipe the sills and frames afterwards, so it does not run back down the pane the next time it rains.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about window cleaning

What does window cleaning cost in Aalborg?

A flat starts at DKK 129 per visit and a house at DKK 149 per visit, exterior. The price depends on the number of windows, and you get a fixed price before we start. Interior cleaning can be added.

How often should windows be cleaned?

Most people choose every 8 weeks. If you live close to a busy road, by the coast or under trees, every 4 weeks gives a noticeably better result. You choose the interval yourself, and you can change it along the way.

What is a pure-water system?

The water is filtered so all minerals and salts are removed, and fed up through a telescopic pole with a soft brush. Because the water is completely pure, it dries without leaving spots or streaks – there is no need to wipe afterwards with a cloth.

Can you reach windows on the first and second floor?

Yes. The telescopic pole reaches several storeys from the ground, so we do not need to put ladders up against the facade. If there are windows we cannot get to, we tell you when we give you the price – not afterwards.

Do you also clean inside?

Yes, interior cleaning is an add-on, and many people add it permanently on top of the exterior a couple of times a year. Sills, mirrors and glass doors can be included.

What if it rains on the agreed day?

Rain does not harm the result – panes cleaned with pure water are not spotted by rain. In strong wind or frost we move the visit and let you know.
