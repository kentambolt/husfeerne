How it works

Four steps from your call to handing over the keys

You don't need to make a plan – we do that. If the new home needs to be made ready too, we can do a deep cleaning in Aalborg before you move in.

  1. 1
    You describe the home

    Square metres, number of rooms and bathrooms, and whether appliances and windows should be included. A couple of sentences on the phone or in the form is enough.

  2. 2
    You get a fixed price in writing

    We work out the job and send you the price before we start. No hourly billing and no extras afterwards.

  3. 3
    We clean according to the checklist

    The same two cleaners arrive with all the equipment and Nordic Swan Ecolabel-certified products. You don't need to be home – we agree access in advance.

  4. 4
    You get a message when we're done

    If anything is nonetheless remarked upon at the handover, we come back and fix it at no charge.

What's included

We clean what gets inspected

The checklist follows what a landlord, an estate agent or a buyer typically goes through at the handover.

Kitchen

  • Appliances inside and out
  • Behind and under fridge and cooker
  • Oven, hob and extractor hood with filter
  • Cupboards and drawers inside and out
  • Sink, taps and tiles descaled

Bathroom and toilet

  • Shower, grout and glass descaled
  • Toilet, basin and taps
  • Mirror, shelves and cupboards inside
  • Tiles, floor and floor drain
  • Washing machine inside and out

The rest of the home

  • Floors washed in every room
  • Skirting boards, door frames and doors
  • Light switches, handles and radiators
  • Windows inside, frames and sashes
  • Wardrobes and built-in cupboards inside
  • Balcony, stairwell and basement or attic storage

Frequently asked questions

Questions about move-out cleaning

What does move-out cleaning cost in Aalborg?

Move-out cleaning is billed at a fixed price from DKK 1,995. The exact price depends on the size and condition of the home, and you get it in writing after a short description – before we start. The price doesn't change afterwards.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Usually we can come within 2–5 business days of your call, and move-out cleaning can often be arranged faster. If you have a fixed handover date, do call right away and we'll put it in the diary.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. Most move-out cleanings we do in an empty home. We agree access in advance, and if we need a key, we always make a written agreement about it.

What if the landlord doesn't approve the cleaning?

Then we come back and fix it at no charge. That's our satisfaction guarantee, and it also applies when it's a landlord, an estate agent or a buyer who has noticed something.

Are windows included in the price?

Windows inside are included, including frames and sashes. Windows outside we agree as an add-on, because it depends on how many windows there are and whether they can be reached – mention it when you describe the home.

Do you also clean basement and attic storage?

Yes, basement and attic storage, balcony and stairwell are on the checklist. If things need clearing or taking away first, just say so – and we include it in the quote.

Do you come outside Aalborg?

Yes. We are based in Aalborg and do move-out cleaning throughout the municipality with no travel fee – apartments in the city centre, Vestbyen, Vejgaard and Aalborg Øst as well as houses in Hasseris, Gug, Skalborg, Svenstrup and Nørresundby. We also cover the rest of North Jutland, including Hjørring, Frederikshavn, Brønderslev, Hobro and Thisted; outside Aalborg Municipality we agree a fee up front.
