What is included

What a stairwell clean covers

We agree the scope in writing, so the residents can see what is done – and how often.

Stairs and corridors

  • Steps and landings washed
  • Handrails and banisters wiped down
  • Skirting boards and trims
  • Lift: floor, walls and mirror
  • Mats vacuumed or beaten
  • Mailboxes and nameplates wiped down

Doors, glass and lighting

  • Entrance door and glass panels
  • Stairwell windows on the inside
  • Light switches and door entry phone
  • Lamps and ceiling fixtures wiped down
  • Window sills
  • Cobwebs in corners and on ceilings

Basement and shared rooms

  • Basement corridors and stairs
  • Bicycle and pram rooms
  • Laundry and drying rooms
  • Bin rooms and container yard
  • Attic corridors
  • Extra tasks by agreement with the board – e.g. cleaning of common rooms and commercial units in the building
For the board

What a housing association should be able to count on

A fixed day in each interval

Stairwell cleaning only becomes a problem when it becomes unpredictable. We set a fixed day, and if we ever have to move it, the contact person is told in advance.

The same two cleaners

They know the stairwell, the door code and where things are kept. The residents can see that it is the same people who come – that makes a difference to how safe a stairwell feels.

Written agreement and fixed price

You get the scope, interval and price in writing, so it can be presented at the general meeting. The price is fixed for the agreement period, and there is no binding beyond it.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about stairwell cleaning

What does stairwell cleaning cost in Aalborg?

From DKK 18 per apartment per visit ex VAT on a fixed agreement. The price depends on the number of floors and apartments, the flooring and the traffic in the stairwell – you get a fixed price after a visit to the stairwell.

How often should a stairwell be cleaned?

Every two weeks suits most stairwells. If there is a lot of traffic, businesses in the building or many families with children, weekly cleaning gives a noticeably better impression. The minimum on a fixed agreement is every 4 weeks.

Who makes the agreement – the board or the property manager?

Either works. We make the agreement with whoever holds the mandate, and there is one contact person with a direct number to us. Residents don't have to go through a customer service line to get a message through.

Can you also clean the windows in the stairwell?

Yes. Windows in stairwells and shared areas can be included in the same agreement, either every visit or a few times a year. We use a pure-water system, so no streaks are left behind.

Do you also do basement corridors, bicycle rooms and the laundry?

Yes, basement corridors, bicycle and pram rooms, the laundry, drying room and bin room can be part of the agreement. Those are often the rooms residents complain about first.

Is there a binding period on the agreement?

No, beyond the agreement period you choose yourselves. The price is fixed for the period, and the scope can be adjusted if your needs change.
