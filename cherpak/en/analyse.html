Competitor analysis – Husfeerne against the Aalborg market

Prepared 7 September 2026 from competitors' own websites, price portals and Trustpilot. Lives in our shared archive at /cherpak – not secret, but not indexed.

Status the same day: the recommendations in section 5 have been implemented on the site – DKK 325/260, a 3-hour minimum, free travel only within Aalborg Municipality, special-service prices raised, and "lowest prices"/"cheapest" removed from all four languages. Business is set at 260 ex VAT (not 275) to keep a single ex-VAT figure across the site. The front-page focus on three core services has not been done – that is a structural change we should decide on together. The figures under "Husfeerne" in the tables below are therefore the old ones.

In short

  1. Husfeerne is the cheapest on all ten services. Not on a few – on all of them. Typically 20–40 % below the local firms and level with the gig platforms (HappyHelper DKK 195, Hjælperne DKK 215).
  2. The price does not cover wages. An employee on the 3F agreement costs about DKK 229–253 per paid hour including pension, holiday pay and fritvalg. At an 80 % billable ratio that is DKK 287–316 per billable hour. Husfeerne charges DKK 212 ex VAT. That is a loss of DKK 75–104 an hour – before travel, products, insurance, admin and the owners' pay.
  3. Positioning and price point in opposite directions. The site promises what the expensive firms promise (employed staff, insurance, the same team, discretion). The price sits where the gig platforms sit – and they have no employer costs.
  4. There is an open space in the market that nobody takes. Everyone sells "peace of mind" and "a regular helper". Nobody in Aalborg sells a fixed, employed, all-female two-person team. The platforms' "regular helper" is a freelancer who can quit tomorrow; the firms deliberately rotate staff to cover absence (Maks says so outright). That is Husfeerne's real differentiator – and it should be priced as premium-light, not as cheapest.
  5. Recommendation: raise the private price to DKK 325/hour incl. VAT (260 ex) now, aiming for 349 once the reviews are there. Introduce a 3-hour minimum per visit. Limit "free travel" to Aalborg Municipality. Fix the four under-priced special services. Remove "lowest prices in North Jutland" from the site and switch to "fixed, transparent prices".

1. Who are we actually competing with?

The Aalborg market falls into three layers, each with its own price logic. Husfeerne has to choose which layer it wants to be compared with – today the site tries to be in all three.

Gig platforms (the price floor, DKK 195–215/hour). HappyHelper and Hjælperne. The helper is self-employed or loosely attached; the platform takes a cut and adds insurance. Hjælperne states itself that wages make up about DKK 150 of the 215. They sell "the same helper every time" and "insured through If" – exactly Husfeerne's peace-of-mind arguments – but on gig terms. Customers choose them when price matters most.

Local firms with employed staff (DKK 270–420/hour). AJ Polering (270 ex), Lindas Rengøringsfirma (325), FSC Rengøring (350 incl. + travel), Maks Rengøring (417.50 incl.), Just Clean, Rengøring Aalborg/JRK. They carry wage costs, drive their own cars and live on regular agreements and dense routes. This is the layer Husfeerne actually belongs to – with employees, insurance and proper conditions – but the price says otherwise.

Specialists (their own niches, their own prices). Nordjysk Møbelrens (sofas/carpets/car seats, local, five-star reviews), Elitebilpleje (mobile car cleaning in Aalborg, DKK 599–1,199), window cleaners such as Sands (from DKK 99) and Din Vinduespudser (DKK 149 first visit, 1,400+ regular customers), Udflytningsgaranti (move-out cleaning with a guarantee). Each owns its niche and has its equipment written off. Husfeerne competes against them on five services at once – with two people.

In addition: Rengøringsenheden, Aalborg Municipality's own provider, cleans for about 3,800 citizens under the free-choice scheme and sells exactly "a fixed contact person" and "peace of mind in your own home". See the opportunity in section 5.

Reading the table. Everyone uses the words "peace of mind" and "regular helper". Nobody can guarantee it: the platforms, because the helper is a freelancer; the firms, because they rotate to cover absence. Husfeerne is the only place in Aalborg where the customer knows exactly which two people are coming, and that they are employed, insured and have signed a key agreement. Add four languages, which nobody else mentions.

That is a strong, honest and lawful argument – and it is exactly the quality the most expensive competitors cannot deliver. So it should not be sold at the lowest price. Customers who want the cheapest choose HappyHelper at 195 anyway; we do not win them with 265. Customers who are about to let someone into their home every two weeks choose on trust – and they pay 325–350 at FSC and Linda today.

On wording: "an all-female team, the same two every time" is the version of the idea that works on customers and holds up legally. Looks and age must not be selling points – they would damage trust with the people who book (often women), and they are illegal to use in job ads.

Why it has not gone wrong yet: as long as the three of us share what is left, the loss hides as low pay to ourselves. With 325 incl. and a 3-hour minimum, home cleaning goes from −75 to about +25 per billable hour for a new employee – thin, but positive, and that is where the trade sits. At 349 it becomes about +45.

Positioning – one layer, not three

  • Choose the firm layer. Husfeerne is a firm with employees. Compare ourselves with FSC, Linda and Maks – not with HappyHelper. Price accordingly, and sell what they cannot: the same two women, every time, employed and insured.
  • Remove "lowest prices in North Jutland" from the special-cleaning section and from all marketing. The phrase invites a price comparison with the platforms, which Husfeerne never wins, and it undermines the trust message. Switch to "fixed, transparent prices – agreed up front". It is true, and it is what Linda wins on.
  • Keep "no commitment", but not "no minimum". Everyone sells "no commitment"; it is a hygiene factor, not a differentiator.
  • Make the service deduction the main argument on price. "325 an hour – about 240 after the service deduction" moves the conversation away from 195 at the platforms, which also qualify for the deduction, but customers do not know that.

Focus – three core services, the rest on request

Two people cannot be specialists in ten things and at the same time own a niche against Nordjysk Møbelrens and Elitebilpleje, who live on one. Recommendation for the front page:

  1. Regular home cleaning – the recurring revenue and what the brand is built for.
  2. Move-out cleaning – high order value, a B2B channel via estate agents and landlords, fixed price.
  3. Business and stairwells – fixed weekly hours, dense routes.

Sofa, car, windows and Airbnb become "ask us" services with prices on request, not front-page banners with lowest-price claims. Pick them up again once they have shown they carry themselves.

Channels the competitors do not use

  • Free-choice provider in Aalborg Municipality. Rengøringsenheden cleans for 3,800 citizens. Private providers can be approved under the free-choice scheme. The municipality pays, the customers are regular, and "a fixed all-female team, peace of mind at home" is exactly what elderly citizens and their children choose on. Look into the approval requirements – it is a slow but very stable channel.
  • Reviews before price. Just Clean wins with 25 five-star reviews and no published price. Nordjysk Møbelrens with four named testimonials from named neighbourhoods. Husfeerne has zero. Ten Google reviews in the first month are worth more than a DKK 60 discount an hour.
  • Languages. No competitor mentions languages at all. Keep it – but it is a door-opener into specific segments (international staff, German holiday guests, Ukrainian families), not an argument for a low price.

Changes to the site if we follow the recommendations

All figures are embedded in index.html (Danish text plus data-en/data-de/data-uk), in the structured data and in the FAQ answers. A price change touches about 40 places. Have it done as one batch so the four languages and the structured data move together – otherwise it says 265 in one place and 325 in another.

6. Caveats and sources

  • Competitor prices are their own published prices or price portals' figures as of 7 September 2026 – not quotes obtained. FSC's figures (DKK 350 incl. + travel; DKK 20/flat) come from search results, as the page could not be fetched in full.
  • Google reviews could not be pulled systematically; only Trustpilot and the firms' own testimonials are included.
  • The wage calculation is based on the Service Agreement rates from 15 March 2026 (Jobpatruljen). The pension share and other employer contributions are estimates marked as such. Have the accountant work it out precisely – but the conclusion does not change at ±5 %.
