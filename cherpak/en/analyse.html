Competitor analysis – where Husfeerne stands in Aalborg

Competitors' own websites, price portals and Trustpilot, checked 7 September 2026. Lives in our shared archive at /cherpak – not secret, but not indexed.

In short

  1. We sit at the bottom of the layer we belong to. The Aalborg market has two price layers: gig platforms (DKK 195–215/hour) and firms with employed staff (DKK 325–420). We are a firm with employees and charge DKK 325 – level with Linda, below AJ, FSC and Maks, and well above the platforms.
  2. The price roughly covers the wage of a new employee on the collective agreement. An employee costs about DKK 229–253 per paid hour; at an 85 % billable ratio, DKK 269–298 per hour we can invoice. We keep DKK 260 ex VAT. The margin comes from dense routes, move-out and special jobs, and from DKK 349 once the reviews are there.
  3. We stand in a spot nobody else occupies. Everyone sells "peace of mind" and "a regular helper". Nobody in Aalborg can guarantee it: the platforms' helper is a freelancer, the firms rotate staff. We are the only place where the customer knows exactly which two people are coming, that they are employed and insured, and that the key agreement is in writing. Plus four languages, which nobody else mentions.
  4. We sell fixed prices, not the lowest prices. Those who want the cheapest choose HappyHelper regardless. Those who let someone into their home every two weeks choose on trust – and pay 325–350 at FSC and Linda today.
  5. What we have not taken on yet: the front page still carries ten services instead of three; free-choice approval in Aalborg Municipality has not been looked into; and we have zero reviews against Just Clean's 25.

1. Who do we compete with?

The market falls into three layers, each with its own price logic.

Gig platforms (the price floor, DKK 195–215/hour). HappyHelper and Hjælperne. The helper is self-employed or loosely attached; the platform takes a cut and adds insurance. Hjælperne states itself that wages make up about DKK 150 of the 215. They sell "the same helper every time" and "insured through If" – our peace-of-mind arguments – but on gig terms. Customers choose them when price matters most.

Local firms with employed staff (DKK 325–420/hour). Lindas Rengøringsfirma (325), AJ Polering (337.50), FSC Rengøring (350 + travel), Maks Rengøring (417.50), Just Clean, Rengøring Aalborg/JRK. They carry wage costs, drive their own cars and live on regular agreements and dense routes. This is our layer.

Specialists (their own niches, their own prices). Nordjysk Møbelrens (sofas/carpets/car seats, local, five-star reviews), Elitebilpleje (mobile car cleaning in Aalborg, DKK 599–1,199), window cleaners such as Sands (from DKK 99) and Din Vinduespudser (DKK 149 first visit, 1,400+ regular customers), Udflytningsgaranti (move-out cleaning with a guarantee). Each owns its niche and has its equipment written off. We meet them on five services at once – with two people.

In addition: Rengøringsenheden, Aalborg Municipality's own provider, cleans for about 3,800 citizens under the free-choice scheme and sells exactly "a fixed contact person" and "peace of mind in your own home". See section 5.

5. How we have positioned ourselves – and what we have not taken on yet

Choices that stand

  • The firm layer, not the platform layer. We compare ourselves with Linda, FSC and Maks – not HappyHelper – and price accordingly. What we sell, they cannot deliver: the same two women, every time, employed and insured.
  • Fixed prices agreed up front – not "lowest prices". "Lowest prices" invites a price comparison with the platforms, which we never win, and undermines the trust message. It is Linda's argument, and it works.
  • No commitment, but a 3-hour minimum. Everyone sells "no commitment"; it is a hygiene factor. The 3-hour minimum and free travel only within Aalborg Municipality, by contrast, are the two rules that lift the billable ratio from about 80 to 85 % – DKK 18 an hour.
  • The service deduction as the price argument. "DKK 325 an hour – about 240 after the service deduction" moves the conversation away from 195 at the platforms, which also qualify for the deduction, but customers do not know that.
  • Special jobs above the hourly rate. Sofa 649, car 549 and move-out from 1,995 sit below the specialists but yield DKK 400–430/hour. They are where we become known, and they carry themselves.

Opportunities we have not taken yet

  • The front page carries ten services. Two people cannot be specialists in ten things and at the same time own a niche against Nordjysk Møbelrens and Elitebilpleje, who live on one. The alternative is three core services on the front page – regular home cleaning, move-out cleaning, business/stairwells – and the rest as "ask us". That is a structural change we need to decide together.
  • Free-choice provider in Aalborg Municipality. Rengøringsenheden cleans for 3,800 citizens; private providers can be approved under the free-choice scheme. The municipality pays, the customers are regular, and "a fixed all-female team, peace of mind at home" is exactly what elderly citizens and their children choose on. The approval requirements have not been looked into.
  • Reviews. Just Clean wins with 25 five-star reviews and no published price. Nordjysk Møbelrens with four named testimonials. We have zero. Ten Google reviews in the first month are worth more than a DKK 60 discount an hour – and they are the precondition for DKK 349.
  • Languages. No competitor mentions languages. It is a door-opener into specific segments (international staff, German holiday guests, Ukrainian families), not an argument for a low price.
